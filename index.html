Behind the World of Terraria

Terraria was created by Andrew “Redigit” Spinks and developed by the independent game studio Re-Logic. First released on May 16, 2011, the game continued to grow through years of updates and support from its community. Whitney “Cenx” Spinks contributed as executive producer and designer, while Scott Lloyd Shelly composed the game’s music.

Welcome to Terraria
